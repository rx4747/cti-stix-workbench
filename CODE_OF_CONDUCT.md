# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while still requiring evidence, respect
privacy and disclosure boundaries, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing sensitive
CTI or vulnerability details without permission are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Until the public repository owner is confirmed,
conduct reports should be made privately to the maintainer rather than in a
public issue.
